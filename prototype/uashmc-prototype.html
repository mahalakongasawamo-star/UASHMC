Non-urgent advice: PhilHealth is separate from your HMO

You can often use both. They cover different parts of your bill and need different documents.

  • PhilHealth — national coverage. Bring your CF1, CF2 and proof of contributions.
  • HMO — your company or personal plan. Usually needs a Letter of Authorization.
  • Senior citizen or PWD — a further 20% discount and VAT exemption applies by law.

Four things worth knowing before your visit

Your plan tier matters. An agreement with your HMO doesn't mean every plan they sell covers every service here.

Your doctor matters. HMOs keep their own list of accredited doctors. Check that your chosen specialist is on your HMO's list.

The procedure matters. A consultation may be covered while a test or an admission needs separate approval.

Give the LOA time. Request your Letter of Authorization as soon as your doctor recommends a procedure — allow 1 to 5 business days.

Outpatient — consultation or test

Usually a direct swipe. Bring your HMO card and one valid government ID. Some plans still need an approval code.

Inpatient — admission or surgery

Bring your HMO card, a government ID, and your LOA. Without a valid LOA you may be treated as a private-paying patient.

Emergency

Come straight in. Emergency care at an accredited facility is normally cashless and does not wait for an LOA — and by law a hospital cannot require a deposit before giving basic emergency care.

How to get your LOA

Seven steps, in the language you prefer.

Urgent advice: Confirm with two people before an admission

  • Call your HMO's own hotline and confirm your plan covers the specific procedure at UASHMC.
  • Then call our HMO and Billing desk on 02 8248 3460 or Viber 0947 875 4949.
  • We can tell you whether an agreement exists and what to bring. Only your HMO can confirm what they will pay.

PhilHealth

National coverage, separate from your HMO. You can often use both.

What to bring
  • Original CF1 — member and employer portions completed
  • Original CF2 — signed by your attending physician
  • Certificate of contributions or proof of premium payment
  • Membership Registration Form, if you are declaring a dependent
  • Valid government ID
Senior citizen and PWD discounts

By law you are entitled to a 20% discount and VAT exemption on medical and dental services, laboratory and diagnostic fees, and your doctor's professional fee.

  • Bring your OSCA-issued senior citizen ID or your PWD ID.
  • Present it at the Billing Section before your bill is finalised.
  • If a promotional discount is larger than the statutory one, you get whichever is higher.
  • How the discount is computed per charge type is confirmed by our Billing Section — we don't publish a worked example, because the treatment differs between services, professional fees and medicines.

Patients & Visitors

Everything you need before you arrive

Admission, rooms, visiting hours, billing, your rights, and how to get your records or a medical certificate.

Immediate action required: Call 911 or go to our Emergency Room now if:

  • There is chest pain or pressure, or difficulty breathing
  • There is heavy bleeding that will not stop
  • Someone has fainted, is confused, or cannot be woken
  • There is sudden weakness, drooping on one side of the face, or slurred speech
  • There has been a serious fall, road accident, burn or head injury
  • A pregnancy has bleeding, severe pain, or reduced baby movement
Call 911 Call UASHMC 02 8248 3460 Directions to the ER

You will not be asked for a deposit first. Under the Anti-Hospital Deposit Law (RA 10932) it is unlawful for any hospital to demand a deposit or advance payment before giving basic emergency care.
